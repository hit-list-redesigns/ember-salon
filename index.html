Good to know before you book.

  • Choose your stylist.

    Select them when you book online or mention them on the phone. Not sure who? We’ll recommend someone for the result you want.

  • Colour comes with a finish.

    Add a cut or blow-wave to any colour service. It gives your stylist the time to finish properly.

  • Book ahead for weekends.

    Weekend and special-occasion appointments go first, so lock in your preferred date early.

  • Give us 24 hours to change.

    Cancel or reschedule by phone or online at least 24 hours ahead. Later changes may incur a fee, and no-shows may be charged the full service.
